Papers

Working paper

The “State” of Misallocation — Decentralisation and Productivity

Presentations: NEUDC (2026); ACEGD, ISI New Delhi (2025)

Abstract

How does the allocation of decision-making authority affect the allocation of productive resources? I study the 2017 abolition of India’s Foreign Investment Promotion Board, which moved approval of foreign investment in sectors requiring government approval from a central inter-ministerial board to individual ministries. Firms in affected industries see assets rise by about 3.3% relative to other firms. The increase is concentrated in firms that were already large and had low marginal revenue products of capital: ex-ante large firms gain roughly 14–16% more capital than smaller firms, and the tails of the within-industry distribution of marginal products widen, consistent with a worsening of capital allocation. Lastly, I find that the increase in misallocation is concentrated in sectors not requiring additional regulatory oversight and scales up with pre-reform exposure to the government-approval route.
